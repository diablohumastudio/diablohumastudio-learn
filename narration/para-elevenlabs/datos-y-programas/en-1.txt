We will look at two ideas that will serve you as a theory basis: the data inside the computer and what a program is.

Now let's look at the data inside the computer.

<break time="2s" />

Data is any stored information: a text, an image, a sound.

<break time="2s" />

Now let's understand the data inside a computer through this analogy with a library.

<break time="2s" />

You and three classmates are working on a few projects. To work on any project, the books on that subject have to reach your hands.

<break time="2s" />

You read the open books of the subject you are working on right now. Very few fit, but reading them is instant.

<break time="2s" />

On the side tables there are closed books for the projects you are working on. A few more books fit, and reaching one is fast.

<break time="2s" />

On the shelves everything fits, but finding a book there is slow: you have to get up.

<break time="2s" />

The rule: the closer to your hands, the faster, but the less fits.
